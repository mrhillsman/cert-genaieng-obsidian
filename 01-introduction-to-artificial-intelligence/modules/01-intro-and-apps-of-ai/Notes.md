**Module 1: Introduction and Applications of AI**
This module is focused on the fundamentals of AI. You will learn the definition of AI and the basics of generative AI. You will explore the impact, applications, and use cases of AI and how it is transforming our lives. Additionally, you will discover common tools and applications of generative AI. You will learn about AI agents and smart assistants and their common use cases. Finally, you will examine how AI is reshaping various industries and sectors.

**Module 2: AI Concepts, Terminology, and Application Domains**
This module discusses the core concepts and techniques of AI. You will learn about deep learning, machine learning, and neural networks. You'll explore generative AI models, including large language models (LLMs), and their capabilities. Additionally, you'll explore the development and application of AI in various domains like natural language processing (NLP), computer vision, and robotics, discovering how advancements in these areas drive diverse innovations and use cases.

**Module 3: Business and Career Transformation through AI**
This module explores the impact of AI, including generative AI, on business transformation. You'll learn about the benefits AI offers to companies and delve into various use cases. Additionally, you'll discover how AI can revolutionize your work and work environment. The module also covers career opportunities in the AI domain.

You'll examine key considerations for organizations looking to adopt AI and learn different frameworks and approaches used for AI implementation. This includes an in-depth look at IBM's AI Ladder framework.

**Module 4: AI: Issues, Concerns, and Ethical Considerations Around AI**
This module focuses on AI ethics and governance and addresses prevalent concerns and issues surrounding the AI landscape. In this module, you’ll learn about the fundamental principles of AI ethics and how to implement them. The module also discusses the specific ethical considerations related to generative AI. You will also learn how AI ethics are enforced through governance and regulations.
